This paper is motivated by Problem~1 of the Balkan Mathematical Olympiad 2017, which asks for all positive integer solutions of
\[
x^3+y^3=x^2+42xy+y^2.
\]
We study the generalized family
\[
x^3+y^3=x^2+kxy+y^2,
\qquad k\in\mathbb Z,
\]
over all integers, and denote by $N_k$ the number of its integer solutions.
We prove that for every pair of positive integers $n$ and $l$ there exists an integer $k>l$ such that $N_k>n$.
On the other hand, for every $k\neq -1,2$ the number of integer solutions is finite and satisfies the explicit bound
\[
N_k \le 4\,\tau\!\left(\left|(k-2)^2(k+1)\right|\right),
\]
where $\tau(m)$ denotes the number of positive divisors of $m$; consequently $N_k\le c\,|k|$ for an absolute constant $c$ and all $|k|\ge 3$.
For the exceptional values $k=-1$ and $k=2$ the equation has infinitely many integer solutions.
Finally, we show that there are infinitely many $k$ for which the equation has exactly four integer solutions.
